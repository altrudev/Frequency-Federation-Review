# Public proposal boundary

Status: PROPOSAL ONLY.

This directory exposes the reviewable coordination contract only:

- Federation Attribution Receipt v0.1 schema;
- Federation Status Registry v0.1 schema;
- synthetic examples;
- semantics and claim ceilings.

It does not expose Frequency-Dev implementation, internal evidence graphs, private validation code, private controls, customer information, billing logic, or proprietary orchestration.

Publication here does not mean federation adoption, active membership, endorsement, or agreement to an economic model.

The proposal is deliberately non-economic. The schemas contain no valid field for revenue allocation, payout, price, weighting, scoring, commission, or payment readiness.

The Status Registry is descriptive. It is not a source of authority. Project-owned entries require owner confirmation, and ACTIVE_PARTICIPANT requires explicit opt-in evidence.

The Attribution Receipt records evidence-backed participation statements only. It does not establish economic value, payment entitlement, causal importance, or external execution.
